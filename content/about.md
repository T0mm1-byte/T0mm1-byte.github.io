I'm a cybersecurity master's student at Università di Pisa, focused on IoT and hardware security. I hold a bachelor's degree in electronics, computer and communication engineering from Università degli Studi di Cagliari, where I built [IDALens](/projects/idalens/), an IDA Pro plugin for interactive cross-reference graphs, as my final project.

My journey began in 2024, when I joined the UniCa sailing team, a university team that designs and builds a Moth-class boat to race against other universities. For a year I was part of the mechatronics team, writing the C libraries that let an STM32 regulate the flap using sensor data.

In 2025 I took part in CyberChallenge, the Italian university training program that ends in an Attack/Defense final. It was my first contact with CTFs, and I fell in love with reverse engineering, probably because I've always liked understanding how things work. I then joined Srdnlen, the CTF team born from CyberChallenge in Cagliari. Over time I moved from rev to hardware, a better fit for my aptitude and skills: a world of protocols, datasheets and low-level code that I enjoy almost every day.

## What I work on

I [solve challenges](/writeups/) across these areas, and I've also [written a few](/challenges/) for Srdnlen CTF:

| Area | Tools |
|---|---|
| Side-channel analysis | ChipWhisperer-Nano |
| Firmware reversing | IDA, Ghidra |
| Protocol and signal analysis | Saleae Logic, PulseView |
| HDL design | Vivado, ModelSim |

The ChipWhisperer-Nano can only target its on-board STM32F0, but it still gives me hands-on experience with the Python libraries and methods used to work with NewAE's widely used tools.

## What's next

I'd like to work in IoT/embedded or automotive security, either by performing side-channel attacks and fault injection on real targets and extracting and analyzing firmware, or by developing secure firmware and FPGA/ASIC cryptographic engines. The EU's Cyber Resilience Act is opening up many paths here, certification labs among them.

I'm currently available for part-time work alongside my studies.
